Privacy Policy for Kinetic Digital Watch Face

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Overview

Kinetic Digital is a Garmin Connect IQ watch face that respects your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the app handles user information.

Data Collection

What We Collect

Kinetic Digital collects and processes the following data locally on your device only:

  1. Device Activity Data:
    • Steps count
    • Active minutes (moderate and vigorous)
    • Calories burned
    • Floors climbed
    • Battery level
  2. User Settings:
    • Unlock code (if entered)
    • Unlock email (if entered)
    • Accent color preference
    • Pro stat selection
    • Ring mode selection
    • Military time format preference

What We Don't Collect

  • We do NOT transmit any data to external servers
  • We do NOT collect personal information beyond what you voluntarily provide
  • We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
  • We do NOT share your data with third parties
  • We do NOT store your data in the cloud

How Data Is Used

Local Processing Only

All data processing happens locally on your Garmin device:

  1. Activity Data: Retrieved from your device's activity monitor to display stats on the watch face
  2. Unlock Email: Used only to generate a local unlock code using a mathematical hash function
  3. Settings: Stored locally to customize your watch face appearance and functionality

Unlock Code Generation

When you provide an email address for unlock code generation:

  • The email is normalized (lowercased, spaces removed)
  • A hash is calculated locally on your device
  • The resulting unlock code is generated using the formula: KD-XXXX-XXXX
  • Your email is never transmitted or stored anywhere except on your device

Data Storage

  • All settings are stored locally using Garmin's property storage system
  • Data persists only on your device
  • Uninstalling the watch face removes all stored settings
  • No data is backed up to external servers

Third-Party Access

Kinetic Digital does NOT:

  • Share data with third parties
  • Use advertising networks
  • Integrate with analytics services
  • Connect to external APIs or services

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View your settings through Garmin Connect or device settings
  • Modify: Change any setting at any time
  • Delete: Uninstall the watch face to remove all data

Children's Privacy

Kinetic Digital does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and does not target children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the watch face after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Garmin's Privacy Policy

This watch face operates within the Garmin Connect IQ ecosystem. Please also review Garmin's Privacy Policy for information about how Garmin handles data.
