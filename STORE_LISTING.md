## Long Description
(Maximum 4000 characters - Current: ~1850 characters)

```
Kinetic Digital is a clean Garmin watch face built around a large digital time display, a crisp date header, compact daily stats, and a progress ring that keeps key information visible at a glance.

FREE MODE FEATURES:
• Large digital time display with date header
• Battery progress ring with tick marks
• Battery, active minutes, and steps tracking
• Gilt accent color
• 12/24-hour time format support

PRO UNLOCK FEATURES:
• Custom accent colors: Gilt, Teal, Blue, Ruby
• Selectable right stat: Seconds, Steps, Active Minutes, Calories, Floors, Battery
• Ring mode options: Battery, Steps Goal, Active Minutes, Day Progress
• Military format option (24-hour with colon separator)

UNLOCK OPTIONS:
Try the watch face with demo codes:
• KINETIC-2026
• PRO-2026
• MJVDIGITAL

Or use email-based unlock codes (generated as KD-XXXX-XXXX format).

SETTINGS:
Configure through Garmin Connect, Garmin Express, or device settings:
• Unlock Code: Enter demo or customer unlock code
• Unlock Email: Optional email for generated unlock codes
• Accent Color: Choose your preferred accent (Pro only)
• Pro Stat: Select right-side stat display (Pro only)
• Ring Mode: Choose outer progress ring type (Pro only)
• Military Format: Force 24-hour time display

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY:
Kinetic Digital emphasizes clarity and hierarchy. The large time display ensures readability at a glance, while the date header and compact stats provide essential information without clutter. The outer progress ring adds visual interest and functional feedback, adapting to show battery, steps, active minutes, or day progress.

SUPPORTED DEVICES:
Compatible with Fenix 8 (43mm, 47mm, Pro 47mm, Solar 47mm, Solar 51mm), Fenix E, Forerunner 570 (42mm, 47mm), Forerunner 970, Venu 4 (41mm, 45mm), Venu X1, Vivoactive 6, Enduro 3, and D2 Mach 2 Pro.

SUPPORT:
For unlock assistance, include your unlock email and device model. Codes are normalized (spaces and hyphens removed). If settings don't update immediately, reopen the watch face or sync from Garmin Connect.

Visit our support page for FAQs, troubleshooting, and feature guides.
```

## Store Listing Best Practices

### Screenshot Tips
1. Use consistent device for main screenshots
2. Show variety of colors and modes
3. Include settings screen to show customization
4. Use realistic but impressive data values
5. Ensure text is readable in thumbnails
6. Capture at actual device resolution
7. No overlays or annotations on screenshots

### Description Tips
1. Lead with key features and benefits
2. Use bullet points for scannability
3. Include demo codes prominently
4. Mention device compatibility
5. Keep language clear and concise
6. Highlight what makes it unique
7. Include support information

### Keyword Strategy
1. Focus on descriptive terms users search for
2. Include style descriptors (clean, modern, minimal)
3. Add functional keywords (stats, battery, steps)
4. Use category-relevant terms (digital, dashboard)
5. Avoid keyword stuffing
